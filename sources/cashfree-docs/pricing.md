What is Cashfree's Festive Offer*?

This offer is built for businesses to get online and live before the festive season hits, without payment costs slowing them down. Are the payment gateway charges fixed? Can I get tailored pricing for my business?

Yes. We can offer customized payment gateway charges if your business has very high average order values or micro-transactions. For custom, pricing get in touch with us here.

When can I start accepting international cards?

Once you upload all payment gateway integration related documents, we verify the details. As your account gets approved, our team will assist you with international card payment activation.

I have a PayPal business account? What are the Cashfree payment gateway charges for enabling PayPal on the checkout page?

If you already have a PayPal business account, you can connect your PayPal account with Cashfree payment gateway account and start accepting international payments from day one. Read our Cashfree PayPal payment gateway integration guide . PayPal integration in Cashfree Payment Gateway is FREE. If you decide to enable PayPal Express Checkout on your Cashfree checkout page, you will be bearing only standard PayPal pricing, Cashfree does not charge any additional payment gateway charges.

Are the payment gateway charges deducted on a per transaction basis?

Yes, the TDR or transaction discount rate is deducted on a per transaction basis. When a transaction is successful, the TDR amount is deducted by Cashfree before settling the proceeds to your bank account.

Are there any additional charges such as setup fee?

With Cashfree, you pay ZERO setup fees.

What are UPI payment gateway charges with Cashfree?

UPI is available as a payment mode on Cashfree Payment Gateway. Your customers can use any UPI app including PhonePe, GooglePay etc to pay. The UPI payment gateway charges are as per the applicable law.
Cashfree offers the widest range of UPI integration such as UPI collect, UPI intent, UPI QR Code, UPI WhatsApp link etc. You can choose from a range of UPI integration and achieve the desired checkout experience.

What is a Platform Fee?

Platform fee is paid by you for services such as Hosted Checkout, Plugins, SDKs, Dashboard, Generation of Reports etc. The Platform Fee is mutually agreed upon between Cashfree and You. Platform fee is a fee other than the fee charged on the transaction for payments instruments.

Frequently asked questions
What exactly is Easy Split?

Easy Split by Cashfree Payments is a split payment gateway for marketplaces that helps marketplaces accept payments from customers, deducts commissions and automatically settles payments to the vendors. Easy Split also takes care of customer refund processing and subsequent adjustment in vendor settlements.

How is vendor settlement managed when there are multiple vendors linked to one transaction?

At the time of adding a transaction, you can assign the appropriate numeric commission amount against each vendor that you add with Cashfree.

Do I have to use Cashfree payment gateway to use Easy Split?

Yes, when you create an Easy Split account, Cashfree Payment Gateway will be automatically activated for your account. All payments collected via the payment gateway are automatically split using the integrated payment flow including refund processing.

How can I define vendor commission structure to split payments?

You can specify an absolute value for the vendor's share in the overall transaction value. The remaining amount (Order Amount - Total Vendor’s share) is considered as your share.

How can I process refunds and manage adjustments with vendors?

You can provide a split while initiating refunds from API or Dashboard. As per your inputs, the corresponding vendor’s balance is debited. This will help take care of any receivables from your vendor.

Can I change the vendor assigned to a transaction?

If the transaction has been marked successful, you will not be able to change the vendor associated with the specific transaction.

Can I have different settlement schedules for different vendors?

Yes, this is possible by modifying vendor’s settlement preferences. By default, pending vendor balances get settled automatically as per the settlement schedule defined by you.

How much time does it take to add a vendor?

Using Easy Split, you can add any number of vendors instantly. Once a vendor is added, Easy Split automatically makes adjustments and settle amounts as per the defined commission structure and settlement cycle.

How much time does it take to transfer money ?

Transfers to bank accounts happen instantly or within a few minutes, depending on your business requirement. Transfers for amounts in excess of ₹ 2,00,000 are restricted by banking hours and can take longer. A payout to UPI-BHIM ID or Paytm wallet is instant.

Does it work on Sundays ?

Yes, it is possible to make bulk payments even on Sundays and bank holidays.

How do I ensure payments are credited to the correct account ?

It is possible to use Cashfree's bank validation feature and API to verify whether an account exists and the name of the account holder at the bank. Transfers to incorrect account details fail instantly so you will know if a payment failed and why.

How can I pay multiple people in bulk ?

This can be done by uploading a simple 4 column excel file to Cashfree containing the account details and amount of all the recipients. It is also possible to use Cashfree's API to trigger bulk payout requests.
