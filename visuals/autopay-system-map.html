Why this exists

The same ₹10,000 rent, two ways to collect it

From 15 Oct, UPI payments above ₹2,000 made to a business carry a charge of 0.4%, capped at ₹300. The business pays it and may not pass it to the customer. Autopay debits carry none.

Payment link, QR or collect request
₹47.20
new UPI charge a month, with GST, paid by management
  • Tenant pays by hand, every month
  • 82% of RentOk's online payments are above ₹2,000
  • Median rent is ₹8,250, so about ₹39 a month at the median
Autopay mandate
₹0
new UPI charge, at any rent
  • Approved once, debits on her day
  • Cashfree's own fee (about ₹15 a debit) is covered by RentOk's ₹49 service charge
  • Nobody tells the tenant about the UPI charge. The pitch: never miss rent

At setup she picks one R46

Two options

Option 1: My rent, every rent period

Mandate
Fixed schedule (monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly). Cashfree debits it.
She approves
A fixed amount: her regular dues for the period, line by line.
Extra bills
Sent as a normal Pay now request, with an offer to switch.
Above ₹15,000
Her bank asks for her UPI PIN on every debit.
Pause
RentOk pauses it at her bank through Cashfree.
Retries
Cashfree, up to 3, an hour apart.

Option 2: All my dues, when they're due Recommended

Mandate
On demand ("as presented"). RentOk raises each debit.
She approves
A limit equal to her regular dues, line by line: ₹10,000 rent + ₹58 platform fee = up to ₹10,058.
Extra bills
Request payment via Autopay, paid after 24 hours unless she pays manually.
Above ₹15,000
Taken in parts of up to ₹15,000, one after another. Same day is still to confirm with Cashfree.
Pause
RentOk raises no debits.
Retries
RentOk, up to 3, timed near salary day.

Regular dues: rent (with GST if charged), fixed recurring charges such as a food package, and the platform fee line. Never late fines, deposits or prepaid recharges.

Three charges, never mixed

Where the money goes

RentOk's service charge
₹49 + GST

Per billed tenant a month, charged to management whatever way the tenant pays. Taken from the payout, or invoiced monthly. R22 R35

Platform fee line
₹58, or ₹49 + GST

The property's charge on the tenant's bill, on by default. ₹49 + GST if the property is GST-registered, ₹58 if not. Same for every payment method. R42 R43

Payment gateway charges
as today

Card, net banking, IMPS, NEFT and similar keep their own charges. The new 0.4% UPI charge on link payments never reaches the tenant: management pays it. R34 R15

Pause R48 R50

A pause is a request the property approves

Any length, up to her agreement end date. A pause made in her UPI app takes effect at once.
